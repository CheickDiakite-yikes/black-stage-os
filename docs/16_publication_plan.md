# 16 Publication Plan

## Purpose

We want to publish findings from building Blackstage, not only ship a product.

The strongest research story is that the product and the build process are both novel:

1. A new interface model for agentic computing.
2. A new method for building complex AI products with AI collaborators.

## Possible publication formats

### Research essays

- The Living Render Field: A New Interface Primitive for Agentic Computing
- Intent Threads: Replacing Apps, Files, and Chats as the Unit of Work
- Visible Agent Labor: Designing Trust for Delegated Computation
- Approval Rituals: Human Control in Agentic Interfaces
- Building Blackstage with Codex: Lessons from AI-Native Product Development

### Technical reports

- Architecture of a Voice-Native Agentic Render Interface
- Event-Sourced UI for Human-Agent Collaboration
- Measuring Coding Agent Performance in Novel Product Development

### Product demos

- "Intent becomes a world" demo video.
- Stage Shell v0 walkthrough.
- Before/after: chat-only vs render-field workflow.

### Month 4+

Publish research study:

> Do Users Trust Agents More When Their Labor Is Visible?

## Rules for publishing

- Be honest about what is simulated.
- Do not reveal private user data.
- Cite relevant sources.
- Include failures.
- Share reproducible methods where possible.
- Do not overclaim generality from tiny samples.
- Make the product demo emotionally compelling but the research claims rigorous.

## Public narrative

The narrative should be bold but grounded:

> Computers were built around manual operation. AI agents make that model obsolete. Blackstage explores what happens when the interface is no longer a place to click through software, but a living field where intent becomes visible, governable work.
